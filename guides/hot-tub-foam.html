How to Get Rid of Hot Tub Foam

Quick answerFoam is surfactant buildup — detergent residue from swimsuits, lotions, deodorant, and body oils — whipped up by the jets. A defoamer knocks it down in minutes but is temporary. The real fix: shock to oxidize the organics, clean the filter, and if the water is more than 3 months old, drain and refill. Prevent it by rinsing suits in plain water and showering before soaking.

Why foam forms

Every soak adds a film of lotion, sunscreen, deodorant, and laundry detergent residue to the water. These are surfactants — molecules that stabilize air bubbles. Once concentration is high enough, jets turn the surface into a bubble bath. Low calcium hardness (very soft water) and old, solids-saturated water both make foaming worse.

Quick fix vs. real fix

  1. Tonight: a squirt of silicone defoamer collapses the foam so you can soak. It treats the symptom only.
  2. Test and balance — check calcium hardness too; below ~150 ppm, raise it with calcium increaser.
  3. Shock with MPS or chlorine to oxidize the dissolved gunk feeding the foam.
  4. Clean the filter with a degreasing filter cleaner — an oily filter re-seeds the water.
  5. Water older than ~3 months, or foam returns within days: drain, do a line flush, and refill. Saturated water cannot be un-saturated.

Prevention that actually works

  • Rinse swimsuits in plain water — never wash your "spa suit" with detergent, or dedicate a suit you only rinse.
  • Quick shower before soaking; it removes most lotion and deodorant load.
  • Shock lightly after heavy-use nights (guests multiply the organic load).
  • Keep the cover clean; a dirty underside drips residue back in.

What you'll need

  • Silicone defoamerInstant knockdown when you want to soak tonight.
  • Non-chlorine shock (MPS)Oxidizes the surfactants and oils causing foam.
  • Filter degreaserAn oil-soaked filter keeps re-seeding foam.
  • Calcium hardness increaserVery soft water foams more easily.

Common questions

Is hot tub foam harmful?

The foam itself isn't dangerous, but it signals a high organic load, which consumes sanitizer quickly. Treat it as a maintenance warning.

Why does my hot tub foam even after a drain and refill?

Usually a filter that was never deep-cleaned, detergent-washed swimsuits, or very soft fill water. Degrease the filter and check calcium hardness.

Does defoamer fix foam permanently?

No. Defoamer collapses bubbles temporarily. The permanent fix is removing the surfactants: shock, filter cleaning, and eventually fresh water.
