How to Fix Cloudy Hot Tub Water

Quick answerCloudy water is almost always one of three things: low sanitizer, poor filtration, or high pH/alkalinity. Test the water first. If sanitizer is low, shock the tub and run the pump on high for 2–4 hours with jets on. If chemistry is fine, clean or replace the filter. Most tubs clear within 24 hours.

Why hot tub water goes cloudy

Cloudiness is suspended particles too small for your filter to grab — dead organics, body oils, lotion, and minerals knocked out of solution. Three root causes account for nearly every case:

  • Low sanitizer. Chlorine or bromine below range lets organics accumulate faster than they're oxidized.
  • Filtration problems. A clogged, oily, or worn filter cartridge passes fine particles straight back to the tub.
  • Unbalanced water. pH above 7.8 or total alkalinity above 120 ppm makes calcium precipitate as a fine haze.

The fix, in order

  1. Test. Check sanitizer, pH, and total alkalinity with fresh test strips. Old strips lie.
  2. Balance first. Get alkalinity to 80–120 ppm and pH to 7.2–7.8. Shocking badly balanced water wastes the shock.
  3. Shock. Add a non-chlorine (MPS) or chlorine shock per label for your tub volume. Leave the cover off for 20 minutes to let oxidation byproducts gas off.
  4. Filter hard. Run the pump on high, jets on, for 2–4 hours. Filtration is what physically removes the haze.
  5. Clean the filter. Rinse thoroughly; if it's been 3–4 months, soak it overnight in filter cleaner. If it's over a year old, replace it.
  6. Still cloudy after 24 hours? A water clarifier can coagulate fine particles so the filter catches them. If the tub is over 3–4 months since a refill, drain and start fresh — old water accumulates dissolved solids no chemical can remove.
Never use pool floc (flocculant) in a hot tub — it settles particles to the floor, which works in a pool you can vacuum but not in a spa.

Common questions

How long does it take cloudy hot tub water to clear?

With correct sanitizer, balanced water, and a clean filter running 2–4 hours on high, most tubs clear within 24 hours. Persistent haze usually means a worn filter or water that's due for a drain.

Can I go in a cloudy hot tub?

Not recommended. Cloudy water usually means low sanitizer, so bacteria may not be controlled. Fix the water first.

Why is my hot tub cloudy right after refilling?

Fresh fill water often carries dissolved metals and minerals. Balance alkalinity and pH, add sanitizer, and run the filter; a metal sequestrant helps if your fill water is hard.
